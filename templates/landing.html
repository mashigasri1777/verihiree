Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Does VeriHire AI auto-reject candidates with suspicious certificates?

No. We believe the recruiter should always remain in control. If an issuing database is unreachable or a format irregularity is detected, VeriHire AI tags the candidate as “Needs Manual Verification” or “Suspicious” and outlines the exact discrepancy so you can request documents or verify manually.

Which resume and certificate formats are supported?

VeriHire AI supports PDF, DOC, DOCX resumes, as well as separate image files (PNG, JPG) for standalone certificate uploads. PyMuPDF and OCR extract text cleanly from both digital and scanned files.

Can I generate formal PDF verification reports for executive audit?

Yes! Every analyzed candidate has a print-ready, downloadable audit report featuring credential stamps, cryptographic hashes, AI confidence ratings, and recruiter sign-offs.
