VERIFICATION WORKFLOW

How VERIRESUME Works

End-to-end automated credential authentication in 4 streamlined steps.

01

Resume Parsing & Extraction

PyMuPDF and python-docx extract candidate contact info, skills, education, and credentials.

02

Multiprocessing Dispatch

Detected certificates are dispatched concurrently across OS worker processes to maximize throughput.

03

TCP Socket Registry Query

Workers communicate over low-level TCP sockets to compare candidate data against official registries.

04

SymPy Math & FP Report

SymPy evaluates the exact authenticity equation and Functional pipelines generate the final audit report.
